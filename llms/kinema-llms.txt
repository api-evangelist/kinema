# Kinema

> Kinema is a social cinema platform and film distribution service that empowers independent filmmakers to release, control, and get paid for their films, while giving hosts the tools to screen movies for their communities, both in person and online.

Kinema helps independent stories thrive. Filmmakers control their release and get paid for it. Hosts and community builders turn any space into a movie house and bring people together around a film. Audiences discover films and attend screenings, virtual or in person.
